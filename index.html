Gas safety

  • Keep cylinders upright, outside or in a well-ventilated area.
  • Check hoses and regulators for cracks, and replace old ones.
  • Test for leaks with soapy water, never with a flame.
  • If you smell gas: close the valve, open windows, switch off flames and call us.
